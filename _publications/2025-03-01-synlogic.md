**First author: Junteng Liu.** This paper presents SynLogic, an approach for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond. Published on arXiv in 2025. The code is available on GitHub.

Co-authors: Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He.
